# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, brand leads, and marketing directors evaluating creative agencies. They land on this site while shortlisting partners for a brand or digital project. They scan fast — within 10 seconds they're either compelled or gone. Context: desktop browser, often during a research session with multiple tabs open.

## Product Purpose

FOURWARD is a creative agency portfolio site. It exists to win the room before a single word is read — the 3D rocket hero is the first impression, the headline is the close. Success = the visitor hits "Start Your Launch" or reaches out directly.

## Brand Personality

Bold. Kinetic. Unapologetic. The name is FOURWARD — always moving, always ahead. Not polished corporate; not chaotic indie. Somewhere between SpaceX launch control and an art-directed editorial spread.

## Anti-references

- Generic Webflow agency templates (rounded cards, pastel gradients, scrolly counters)
- AI-purple / violet-to-cyan "tech startup" default (the current palette — escape it)
- Dark mode SaaS dashboards posed as agency sites
- Overcrowded hero sections with feature grids and social proof

## Design Principles

1. **The rocket lands first.** Motion and 3D presence must earn attention before type does. Static fallbacks exist but are not the designed experience.
2. **One accent, total commitment.** A single electric color used with discipline beats a gradient palette used everywhere.
3. **Weight is hierarchy.** Use font weight and scale to create order — not color, not size alone.
4. **Earn every element.** If a visual component doesn't move the visitor closer to "Start Your Launch," cut it.
5. **Space is premium.** Generous negative space signals confidence. Crowded layouts signal anxiety.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum. Large display text (≥18px bold) at 3:1 contrast minimum; body copy at 4.5:1.
- All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No motion-gated content — elements must be visible in their static default state.
